Privacy Policy

Snap! Screen Translator is designed as a private iPhone utility. Normal translation use is centered on local screenshot text recognition, local translation workflows, and App Store managed purchase access.

Short version: the current iOS build does not require an account, does not use ads or behavioral tracking, and declares no collected data types in its Apple privacy manifest.

Local first

Screenshots, recognized text, and translated text are intended to be processed on your device during normal use.

No tracking

The current iOS build is not configured for advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling.

Support by email

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include so we can respond.

1. Information Processed by the App

Depending on how you use Snap! Screen Translator, the app may process:

  • Screenshots or images you choose to translate.
  • Text recognized from those images.
  • Translated text and layout information used to render the translated preview.
  • Your selected target language, source language mode, appearance setting, shortcut setup state, and offline model choices.
  • Subscription entitlement state provided through Apple App Store systems so the app can determine access.

2. No Collected Data Types in the Current iOS Build

The current iOS app privacy manifest declares no collected data types and tracking is disabled. This means the app is not designed to collect personal data through analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs in the current build.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To recognize text in screenshots and images you provide.
  • To translate text and display the translated preview.
  • To remember local preferences such as target language, appearance, and offline model state.
  • To confirm subscription access, restore purchases, or show current access status.
  • To respond to support requests you send by email.

4. On-Device Processing

Snap is designed around on-device screenshot translation. Screenshots, recognized text, and translated text are intended to remain on your device during normal use unless you choose to share output using system sharing tools or include information in a support email.

5. Local Storage and Retention

The app may store preferences, subscription access state, shortcut setup state, offline model state, and similar operational settings on your device. Offline language models are stored locally until you remove them in the app or remove the app from your device.

Screenshots passed into the translation flow are intended to be retained only as needed to complete the requested translation workflow.

6. App Store Purchases

Subscriptions and billing are handled by Apple through the App Store. Snap receives purchase or entitlement status needed to unlock access, but we do not receive your App Store password or payment card details.

7. Support Email

If you email support, your email address and the information you include will be used to respond, troubleshoot, and keep a support history related to your request. Avoid sending screenshots or personal information unless it is necessary for your support request.

8. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not require account creation for normal use.
  • We do not use the current iOS build for behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking.

9. Sharing

We may disclose information only when necessary to comply with law, enforce rights, protect users, respond to a request you make, or work with platform providers such as Apple for purchases and app distribution.

10. Your Choices

  • Delete screenshots from Photos or Files using normal iOS controls.
  • Remove downloaded offline models inside the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove local app data according to iOS behavior.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple subscription settings.
  • Contact support with privacy questions or requests.

11. Children

Snap! Screen Translator is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will update the effective date above.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, contact snapappsupport@gmail.com.
