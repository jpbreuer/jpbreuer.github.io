A short setup, then a fast translation habit.

The app supports manual screenshot import, but it is designed around a shortcut-based iPhone workflow for moments when you want the translation instantly without switching contexts.

  1. 1 Pick your target language. Snap detects the source language automatically and translates into the language you choose.
  2. 2 Install the Snap shortcut. Follow the in-app setup screen and connect the shortcut to your preferred iPhone trigger.
  3. 3 Capture and review. Trigger the flow, let Snap recognize the screenshot text, then review the translated overlay.
Privacy posture

Useful screenshot translation should not need an account or ad profile.

Snap is built as a utility. In the current iOS build, normal translation use is centered on local processing and App Store managed purchases.

Privacy Details
  • No account is required for normal use.
  • No advertising SDK or behavioral tracking is used in the build.
  • The app privacy manifest declares no collected data types.
  • Screenshot text and translation output are intended to be processed locally unless you choose to share them.
  • Support email only receives the information you decide to send.

Unlimited access is managed by the App Store.

The app offers weekly, monthly, and yearly auto-renewing plans. Eligible accounts may see a 3-day free trial or introductory offer before confirmation. Restore Purchases is available in the app if access does not update automatically.

Quick answers

Does Snap require an account?

No. Normal app use does not require creating a Snap account. Purchases are handled by the App Store.

Can screenshots contain private information?

Yes. Only translate screenshots you are comfortable processing on your device, and do not send private screenshots to support unless you choose to include them.

Where do I get help?

Use the support page or email snapappsupport@gmail.com with your device, iOS version, app version, target language, and what happened.
